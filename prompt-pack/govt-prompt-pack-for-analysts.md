## How to Use the Pack

## 如何使用本合集

1. **Replace brackets […]** with your own program names, focus area, or data sources.

1. **替换方括号 […]** 为您自己的项目名称、关注领域或数据来源。

2. **Attach** any spreadsheets, documents, or links ChatGPT should reference.

2. **附加** 任何 ChatGPT 应参考的电子表格、文档或链接。

3. **Specify output style** (e.g., "bullet points," ".csv file," or "visualization").

3. **指定输出样式**(例如,"要点"、".csv 文件"或"可视化")。

4. **Review every draft** for accuracy, compliance, privacy, and accessibility before sharing.

4. **审查每份草稿** 的准确性、合规性、隐私性和可访问性,然后再分享。

## Simple steps for writing a good prompt

## 撰写优质提示词的简单步骤

**1. Outline the task** Be clear about what you need ChatGPT to do.

**1. 概述任务** 清楚说明您需要 ChatGPT 做什么。

→ _Example_: _Summarize last quarter's program participation data and suggest policy or operational adjustments for the next quarter._

→ _示例_:_总结上季度的项目参与数据,并为下季度建议政策或运营调整。_

**2. Give helpful context** Add any background (or documentation) that will help.

**2. 提供有用的背景** 添加任何有助于理解的背景(或文档)。

→ _Example_: _Use data from our Q2 outreach and enrollment report submitted to the Department of Health Services._

→ _示例_:_使用我们提交给卫生服务部的第二季度外展和注册报告中的数据。_

**3. Describe your ideal output** Tell ChatGPT how you'd like the response.

**3. 描述您理想的输出** 告诉 ChatGPT 您希望响应如何呈现。

→ _Example_: _Write it as a formal executive summary._

→ _示例_:_以正式的执行摘要形式撰写。_
