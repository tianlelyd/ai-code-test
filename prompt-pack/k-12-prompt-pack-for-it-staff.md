### **Sample prompts to make the most of ChatGPT Edu**

### **充分利用 ChatGPT Edu 的示例提示词**

This set of prompts is designed to support K–12 IT staff with everyday tasks like tech support, system communications, policy writing, and data management. Each prompt serves as a flexible template to help you generate clear, user-friendly content, whether you're crafting a help desk reply, drafting cybersecurity reminders, or documenting device policies. We recommend editing these prompts to reflect your district's tools, systems, and communication style. Use them as a foundation to save time, reduce repetitive work, and deliver consistent, approachable tech guidance to staff, students, and families.

本提示词集旨在支持 K-12 IT 人员处理日常任务,如技术支持、系统沟通、政策撰写和数据管理。每个提示词都是一个灵活的模板,帮助您生成清晰、用户友好的内容,无论您是撰写帮助台回复、起草网络安全提醒,还是记录设备政策。我们建议编辑这些提示词以反映您所在学区的工具、系统和沟通风格。将它们作为基础,节省时间、减少重复工作,并为员工、学生和家庭提供一致、易于理解的技术指导。

### **Help Desk & Troubleshooting Support**

### **帮助台与故障排除支持**

**Prompt:** "Create a user-friendly help guide for teachers at Greenfield High School on how to connect student Chromebooks to the school Wi-Fi network. Include screenshots, simple language, and common troubleshooting tips."

**提示词:** "为绿野高中的教师创建一个用户友好的帮助指南,说明如何将学生 Chromebook 连接到学校 Wi-Fi 网络。包括屏幕截图、简单的语言和常见的故障排除技巧。"

**Prompt:**"Write a response to a support ticket from a teacher unable to access the district's learning management system. Include step-by-step guidance and a friendly tone."

**提示词:**"回复一张来自无法访问学区学习管理系统的教师的支持工单。包括逐步指导和友好的语气。"

### **Cybersecurity Awareness**

### **网络安全意识**

**Prompt**: "Draft a short reminder email to all staff about best practices for avoiding phishing attacks, especially during state testing season. Include 3–5 bullet points with examples."

**提示词**: "起草一封简短的提醒邮件,向所有员工介绍避免网络钓鱼攻击的最佳实践,尤其是在州测试季节期间。包括 3-5 个要点并附带示例。"

**Prompt:** "Compose an announcement for students and families about a recent district-wide password reset. Explain why it's happening, how to reset passwords, and how to contact tech support."

**提示词:** "撰写一则面向学生和家庭的公告,说明最近的学区范围密码重置。解释发生的原因、如何重置密码以及如何联系技术支持。"

### **Technical Documentation & Policy Writing**

### **技术文档与政策撰写**

**Prompt:**"Write a one-page Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for middle school students using school-issued devices. Include sections on appropriate use, data privacy, and consequences for misuse."

**提示词:**"为使用学校发放设备的中学生撰写一页的可接受使用政策 (AUP)。包括适当使用、数据隐私和滥用后果等部分。"

**Prompt:**"Summarize the key points of the district's data privacy policy for a staff-facing FAQ. Keep it clear and concise, and address common teacher concerns."

**提示词:**"为面向员工的常见问题解答总结学区数据隐私政策的关键要点。保持清晰简洁,并解决教师的常见疑虑。"

### **System Updates & Communication**

### **系统更新与沟通**

**Prompt:** "Draft a notice to all school staff about an upcoming scheduled maintenance window for the grading system. Include the date, time, expected impact, and contact info for questions."

**提示词:** "起草一则通知,告知所有学校员工即将进行的评分系统计划维护窗口。包括日期、时间、预期影响以及问题联系信息。"

**Prompt:**"Write an all-call message script for the principal to use in notifying families about a temporary outage of the school's online portal. Include alternate access instructions and expected resolution time."

**提示词:**"撰写一个全员通话消息脚本,供校长使用,通知家庭学校在线门户的临时中断。包括备用访问说明和预期解决时间。"

### **Data Management & Reporting**

### **数据管理与报告**

**Prompt:** "Generate a checklist for school registrars to verify data accuracy before uploading student enrollment records to the state database. Include steps for checking duplicates, demographic fields, and enrollment dates."

**提示词:** "为学校注册员生成一个检查清单,以在将学生入学记录上传到州数据库之前验证数据准确性。包括检查重复项、人口统计字段和入学日期的步骤。"

**Prompt:** "Write a query or formula for identifying students with more than 10 absences in a Google Sheet. Provide an explanation of how it works for school data managers."

**提示词:** "编写一个查询或公式,用于在 Google 表格中识别缺勤超过 10 次的学生。为学校数据管理员提供其工作原理的解释。"
