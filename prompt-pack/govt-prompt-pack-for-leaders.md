# Prompt Packs

# 提示词合集

A quick-start guide for leaders at any level of government who just received ChatGPT access and wonder, "How can this help me today?"

面向刚获得 ChatGPT 访问权限的各级政府领导者的快速入门指南——"这如何能在今天帮助我?"

## Why It Matters

## 为何重要

Whether you serve in a national government, regional authority, or local government agency, you balance policy, budgets, crises, and citizen expectations—often simultaneously. Generative AI won't replace your expertise, experience or judgment, but it can **speed the drafting, analysis, and coordination work that fills your calendar**. The prompt-pack below offers copy-ready starters you can paste into ChatGPT right now, each paired with a concise "So What?" that explains its payoff.

无论您服务于国家政府、地区当局还是地方政府机构,您都需要平衡政策、预算、危机和公民期望——往往是同时进行。生成式人工智能不会取代您的专业知识、经验或判断力,但它可以 **加速填满您日程的起草、分析和协调工作**。以下提示词合集提供了可直接复制的入门模板,您现在就可以粘贴到 ChatGPT 中,每个模板都配有简明的"有何用处?"说明其价值。

## How to use the pack

## 如何使用本合集

1. **Replace brackets […]** with your own program names, jurisdiction, or data sources.

1. **替换方括号 […]** 为您自己的项目名称、管辖区或数据来源。

2. Attach any spreadsheets, reports, or links ChatGPT should reference.

2. 附加任何 ChatGPT 应参考的电子表格、报告或链接。

3. Specify output style (e.g., "bullet points," "one-page brief," or "public-facing summary").

3. 指定输出样式(例如,"要点"、"一页简报"或"面向公众的摘要")。

4. Review every draft for accuracy, suitability, compliance before sharing.

4. 在分享前审查每份草稿的准确性、适用性和合规性。

## Simple steps for writing a good prompt

## 撰写优质提示词的简单步骤

**1. Outline the task** Be clear about what you need ChatGPT to do.

**1. 概述任务** 清楚说明您需要 ChatGPT 做什么。

→ _Example_: _Summarize last quarter's program participation data and suggest policy or operational adjustments for the next quarter._

→ _示例_:_总结上季度的项目参与数据,并为下季度建议政策或运营调整。_

**2. Give helpful context** Add any background (or documentation) that will help.

**2. 提供有用的背景** 添加任何有助于理解的背景(或文档)。

→ _Example_: _Use data from our Q2 outreach and enrollment report submitted to the Department of Health Services._

→ _示例_:_使用我们提交给卫生服务部的第二季度外展和注册报告中的数据。_

**3. Describe your ideal output** Tell ChatGPT how you'd like the response.

**3. 描述您理想的输出** 告诉 ChatGPT 您希望响应如何呈现。

→ _Example_: _Write it as a formal executive summary._

→ _示例_:_以正式的执行摘要形式撰写。_
